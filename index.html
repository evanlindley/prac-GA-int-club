Questions

Who can present?
Anyone is welcome to present on any international culture or topic they want.
How should I structure my presentation?
Presentations have no requirements; make them as formal or informal as you want.
Do I have to make presentations to be a part of the club?
No presentation is required; all we ask is engagement and attendence.
Can I get political?
Of course! GA International Club is all about sharing perspectives, and politics are incredibly important around the world.

Interested?

Let us know! You can come present or simply listen; everyone is welcome at GA International Club!
